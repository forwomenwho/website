About

About the magazine

For Women Who is a weekly magazine about work, life, taste and everything we're still figuring out. A new issue comes out every Sunday.

What we publish

Work
Careers, ambition, money, and the things worth having on your desk.
Life
Routines, procrastination, friendships, comparison and standards.
Style
What to wear, what to buy, and how to get dressed on a Tuesday.
Beauty
Products we'd put in our own bag, with an honest note on each.
Culture
Books, women worth knowing, and things worth noticing.

How we write

We try to write the way an intelligent friend talks. Some pieces are useful, some are funny, and some are only very accurate.

None of them are rules for being a better woman.
